# Mac transport calibration and baseline

**Branch:** `perf-bench`. **Baseline:** 40 trials, five 30-second repeats per case, 20 Hz probes. Both profiles add 30 ms RTT. Candidate/reference binaries and measurement sources stayed frozen.

**Quality:** all 40 trials verified data integrity; 35 had valid timing and completed probe service; 3 were timing-invalid and 2 failed scheduled-probe service. No artifacts were altered or missing. Calibration is **not approved**: 35/36 independent samples passed, but one clean unloaded sample exceeded existing timing/RTT limits.

## Verified goodput and latency

Diagnostic medians across all five repeats, including flagged runs. Goodput is payload Mbit/s. RTT is injected-to-observed echo latency, in milliseconds. Latency columns are medians of per-trial percentiles, not pooled percentiles.

| Profile | Upload | Download | Mixed download | Unloaded p95 / p99 | Mixed p95 / p99 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Clean 10 Mbit/s | 0.270 | 0.266 | 0.100 | 32.56 / 32.81 | 1330.43 / 1335.28 |
| 10 Mbit/s, 0.05% loss, +/-1 ms jitter | 0.263 | 0.256 | 0.103 | 33.04 / 33.68 | 1282.31 / 1331.37 |

The impaired unloaded p99 ranges from **33.44 to 228.38 ms** across repeats; its median alone conceals the worst run. Clean mixed p99 is 1334.36–1337.03 ms, and impaired mixed p99 is 1288.10–1429.79 ms. Full ranges and the strict timing-valid/service-complete subset are in the retained detailed report.

Scheduled-to-response latency also includes driver delay: clean unloaded p95/p99 38.14/38.52 ms, impaired unloaded 37.54/38.47 ms; clean mixed 1334.31/1337.41 ms and impaired mixed 1285.51/1333.72 ms.

## Failures

- Clean upload, seed 47: reverse-path relay p99 lateness **2.065 ms**, over the **1.5 ms** limit.
- Impaired download, seeds 11 and 29: forward-path relay p99 lateness **2.416 ms** and **2.132 ms**, over the same limit.
- Impaired unloaded probes, seed 101: **one** scheduled probe was never injected; p99 RTT **228.38 ms**.
- Impaired mixed, seed 101: **two** scheduled probes were never injected.
- All three missed injections occurred inside the measurement phase. Every injected probe was observed; probe response timeouts and confirmed data corruption/loss after drain were **zero**.

The benchmark and CI gate returned exit status **1**, preserving all failures rather than reporting a clean pass.

## Calibration evidence and limits

- Independent saturated wire rates were **9.959–9.967 Mbit/s** for the 10 Mbit/s cap; paced rates, deterministic loss IDs and packet CRCs/sequence accounting passed.
- Raw relay headroom passed at a **6000 packets/s sustained target**, averaging **6720 packets/s with 4x short offered bursts**. Baseline counter-sample peak was **109.4 packets/s**, giving over **60x** observed sample-rate headroom. Exact native 10-ms packet bursts were not timestamped.
- Native direct capacity checks: all 15 passed; median upload/download **363/363 Mbit/s**, duplex about **299 Mbit/s each way**. This demonstrates CPU/driver headroom.
- Native counter/observer on/off study: all 12 passed; median upload throughput effect **-1.03%**, mixed effect **+0.30%**. Raw observer study added median **3.91%** CPU with no delivered-rate decrease. These are observed effects from three paired runs, not universal overhead bounds.
- One raw clean unloaded sample failed dispatch/RTT criteria (relay p99 **1.836 ms** versus 1.5 ms; observed RTT p99 **35.72 ms**). No threshold was relaxed and no lab receipt was approved.
- The reference uses this same synchronous sender. Direct CPU headroom does not remove its stop-and-wait limit at 30 ms RTT. Download goodput describes the association and does **not** isolate DUT receiver capacity. Mixed tails also include application echoes queued behind bulk writes.
- macOS thermal/performance warning status was unavailable; AC power and available ambient load/process evidence were retained.

## Native UDP2 campaign

Twenty trials used the production 32 KiB queue, seeds 11/29/47/71/101,
10 Mbit/s in each direction, and independent loss affecting DATA, ACKs and
retries. Warmup/measurement/drain were 5/30/12 seconds. No accepted lab
calibration receipt is implied.

| Loss each direction | Full streams verified | Integrity-verified payload goodput Mbit/s (N) | Timing-qualified observed p95 / p99 ms (N) | Expected / injected / observed probes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.05% control | 5/5 | 3.540 (5) | 79.339 / 304.594 (5) | 3500 / 3454 / 3454 |
| 25% | 5/5 | 2.945 (4) | 744.438 / 1049.142 (4) | 3500 / 3473 / 3473 |
| 50% | 0/5 | Unavailable | Unavailable (0) | 3500 / 227 / 126 |
| 70% | 0/5 | Unavailable | Unavailable (0) | 3500 / 29 / 5 |

Values are medians of per-trial statistics. Rates and latency in this table
exclude harness-invalid trials; latency covers surviving responses only.
All **20 SUT outcomes are FAIL**: control and 25% missed 46/27 scheduled
probes despite observing every injected probe; 50%/70% suffered terminal
native failures. Ten trials verified complete payloads. The separate payload
goodput metric does not promote their SUT outcomes; COMPLETE-qualified
goodput remains unavailable. Nine trials have harness qualification failures:
25% seed 11, 50% seeds 29/47/101 and all 70% seeds. No benchmark deadline
caused a stop. [All raw outcomes and diagnostic latency](../results/pr13608-loss-20261013.md)
are retained in [JSON](../results/pr13608-loss-20261013.json) and
[CSV](../results/pr13608-loss-20261013.csv).

Against the [historical baseline](LOSS-STRESS-20261013.md), control throughput
fell 12.8% (4.059→3.540 Mbit/s), p95/p99 rose from 67.1/96.6 to
79.3/304.6 ms, and probe service regressed. At 25%, complete delivery improved
from zero to five streams, with probe-service failures still present.
These combined-patch historical comparisons do not isolate ACK batching
from RTO behavior, and equal seeds do not preserve identical logical losses
after traffic changes.

At 50%, median first failure was 3.780 seconds. Ten-retry exhaustion that
early necessarily includes fast recovery: the 300 ms exponential timer alone
cannot reach ten retries before 273.3 seconds. Subsequent receiver logs show
sixteen-second channel-gap failures. At 70%, median first failure was
19.060 seconds, diagnostic only because every trial was timing-invalid.

## Windows receive-direction method

The [OpenThinRDP-method adaptation](UPSTREAM-METHOD.md) used Linux Xvfb,
1280×800 progressive GFX with AVC disabled, and upstream Windows coloured
noise. Ingress netem applied loss to all IPv4 from the VM without a bandwidth
cap; packets ≥512 bytes additionally received 1 ms delay and 70% reordering.
Veth replaced IFB, and upstream ICMP verification was omitted.

| Requested loss / duration | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 25% / 120 s | Session ended after **8.030 s**, exit 147 |
| 50% / 120 s | NOT_RUN after the first exit |
| 70% / 120 s | NOT_RUN after the first exit |

The client recorded `ERRCONNECT 0x0002000D` and **UDP channel receive window
overflow**. This is a collision in the **256-slot channel reassembly map**;
the evidence does not establish a peer LogWindow or DataSeq ACK-window
overflow. Stopping after exit follows upstream's rule. Network counters
confirmed completed-attempt drop fractions of **25.218% large / 24.947% small**.

All seven Windows PDH metrics were available in the zero-loss preflight,
which observed about 8.85 server output FPS while the large-packet delay and
reordering treatment remained enabled. The loss-phase failure happened
before a complete ten-second counter interval; its FPS is unknown. This
method measures neither verified application goodput nor physical client FPS.
[Live report](../results/pr13608-upstream-method-20261013.md) ·
[JSON](../results/pr13608-upstream-method-20261013.json).

The results support keeping this branch for review, with three next targets:
safe receive-map backlog/span handling, the shared fast/timer retry budget,
and control-profile latency/backpressure. They do not establish a broad
performance improvement or justify integration yet.

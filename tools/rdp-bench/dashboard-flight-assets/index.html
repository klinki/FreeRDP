01 / TREATMENT

Outstanding DATA packets

The limit is the number of DATA payload chunks awaiting acknowledgement. It is separate from UDP wire size, application-record length and byte-queue capacity.

The encoded sequence span is tracked separately because retransmission aliases and holes can make it larger than the outstanding count.

03 / MEASUREMENT

Goodput requires integrity

Application payload counts only after complete byte, hash, CRC-record and order verification. Partial streams retain diagnostic prefix evidence and never become verified goodput.

p95/p99 are medians of per-trial percentiles for measurement-scheduled probes in the selected cohort. RTT runs from the send attempt to the observed response and includes admission blocking. Probe totals include warmup and measurement across all intact trials; packet peaks include the whole endpoint lifetime, including warmup and drain. Missing probes remain separate; observed latency describes surviving responses.

04 / QUALIFICATION

Failures remain visible

Qualified complete trials require intact evidence, complete service, healthy endpoints and valid relay timing. Probe-only failures with verified streams form a separate cohort.

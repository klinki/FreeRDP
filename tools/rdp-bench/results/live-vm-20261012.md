## Measurement limits

- Live interaction, render timings and transport-only goodput are separate measurements.
- UDP-only proxy profiles leave TCP control/fallback unshaped; inspect each trial's network_scope.
- All planned trials, failures and incomplete evidence remain visible.
- Reference/candidate comparisons use recorded provenance variant IDs and matching phase/case/configuration/seed; all-observed values remain diagnostic.
- Failed preflight statuses remain distinct phases without numeric measurement evidence.
- Visible input-response percentiles describe observed probes; failures, misses, unobserved/censored IDs and timeouts stay separate.
- UDP-only shaping does not certify a full-RDP 10 Mbit/s profile. TCP and graphics routing require their recorded evidence.
- Network traffic and SDL texture upload bytes are not verified RDP application goodput.
